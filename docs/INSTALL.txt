MAC ACTION RECORDER - INSTALL (about 2 minutes)

1. Drag "Mac Action Recorder" onto "Applications" (in this window).
   Then eject this disk image (Finder sidebar, click the eject button).

2. Open Applications and double-click "Mac Action Recorder".
   macOS will probably say it cannot verify the app. That is expected: this
   app is not notarized by Apple (the developer has no paid Apple Developer
   account). Click "Done", then:
     - System Settings > Privacy & Security > scroll down to "Security"
     - Click "Open Anyway" next to "Mac Action Recorder was blocked..."
     - Enter your password or use Touch ID, then click "Open Anyway" again.
   (The Open Anyway button appears for about an hour after your first attempt
   to open the app. If it is gone, try opening the app once more.)

   Alternative, in Terminal:
     xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/Mac Action Recorder.app"

3. The Welcome window opens. Click "Set Up This Mac". The app signs itself
   with a certificate made on your Mac and reopens. If macOS asks whether
   "codesign" may use a key, choose "Always Allow".

4. Back in the Welcome window, allow Input Monitoring and Accessibility
   (the buttons open the right System Settings pages), then click Enter.

Requirements: macOS 13 or newer. Nothing else is installed or needed.
Optional: the on-device AI helper needs macOS 26 or newer with Apple
Intelligence turned on.

Everything stays on your Mac. The only network use is an optional check for
new versions (you can turn it off in Settings > Updates).
